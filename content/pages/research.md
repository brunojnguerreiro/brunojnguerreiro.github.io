## CAPTURE

<img src="{static}/images/research/capture.jpg" alt="Shuttle drones launching and capturing fixed-wing target drones" class="float-image-right">

**Shuttle drone for launch and capture in cooperative and non-cooperative
scenarios** · [Project website](http://capture.isr.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/)

This project addresses the scientific and technological challenges of using
shuttle drones to launch and capture other vehicles or objects. In a first,
cooperative scenario, the shuttle drones cooperate with the vehicles to be
launched or captured, either relaying information about their planned motion or
actively synchronizing their motion to make the manoeuvre easier. This enables
specialized vehicles for a given task, for instance long-endurance fixed-wing
drones with vertical take-off and landing provided by shuttle drones. A second,
non-cooperative scenario involves objects or drones that may not cooperate,
passively or actively, where shuttle drones act as a security measure to remove
them from restricted areas.

The challenges include optimal and cooperative trajectory planning for teams of
heterogeneous vehicles; cooperative, hybrid and distributed control for critical
rendezvous manoeuvres; cooperative and distributed estimation of the motion of
the vehicles and the environment; and estimation, control and planning based on
differential games for the non-cooperative scenario.

## REPLACE

<img src="{static}/images/research/replace-3d_relay.jpg" alt="Drone relay network for parcel delivery with 3-D relay manoeuvres" class="float-image-right">

**Fast delivery in urban environments using drone RElays: PLAnning, Control,
and Estimation** · [Project website](http://replace.isr.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/)

This project addresses the scientific and technological challenges of fast
parcel delivery in urban environments using drones. Since drones have severe
endurance and range limitations, the project uses drone relays to deliver
parcels beyond the range of a single drone. This raises challenges in the
planning of optimal and efficient trajectories for a team of drones deployed
throughout a city, in the cooperative control of several drones for parcel
transportation and relay manoeuvres, and in the estimation of the vehicles and
parcel locations during transportation and relay manoeuvres. The research
starts with single-vehicle control and estimation and progressively evolves to
cooperative control, estimation and planning for coordinated relay
transportation.

<div style="clear:both"></div>

- **References:** FCT PTDC/EEI-AUT/32107/2017; Portugal 2020
  LISBOA-01-0145-FEDER-032107. Funded by Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia
  (FCT), Lisboa 2020, Portugal 2020 and FEDER.
- **Partners:** [ISR Lisboa](http://isr.tecnico.ulisboa.pt), LARSYS, IST-ID (main
  contractor); [IDMEC](http://www.idmec.ist.utl.pt/), LAETA.
- **Team:** Bruno Guerreiro (PI, ISR; FCT/UNL), Rita Cunha (Co-PI, ISR/IST),
  Paulo Oliveira (IDMEC), Carlos Silvestre (ISR/IST).

**Main goals:** fundamental science in robotics and control; novel strategies
for parcel transportation in urban environments, using a single UAV or
multiple UAVs with in-flight relay manoeuvres; new planning, control and
estimation methods that are reliable and independent of external sensors and
processing units, using hybrid systems for improved robustness.

**Application scenarios:**

- *Single vehicle in urban and dynamic environments:* parcel pick-up and
  delivery, SLAM in dynamic environments, and control with interaction with the
  environment.
- *Multiple vehicles with simple relay manoeuvres:* cooperative delivery where
  one drone only needs to be above the other for the relay, with distributed
  real-time predictive algorithms.
- *Multiple vehicles with 3-D relay manoeuvres:* fast cooperative delivery with
  optimized, possibly acrobatic, relay manoeuvres, using full 3-D trajectory
  planning and control.
