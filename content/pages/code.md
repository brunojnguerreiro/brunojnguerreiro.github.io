Software, datasets and templates that I make available, mostly related to drone
control, estimation and simulation. All my public repositories are on GitHub:
[github.com/brunojnguerreiro](https://github.com/brunojnguerreiro).

## nova-drone-arena

Software for the NOVA-DroneArena testbed, the experimental arena for indoor
drone flight at NOVA FCT. *C++*

[Repository](https://github.com/brunojnguerreiro/nova-drone-arena)

## replace-dataset1

Dataset 1 of the [REPLACE](http://replace.isr.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/) project, for
simultaneous localization, mapping and moving object tracking (SLAMMOT).
*MATLAB*

[Repository](https://github.com/brunojnguerreiro/replace-dataset1)

## nova-ieec-m262

Class repository with a simple drone simulation, used in teaching at NOVA FCT.
*MATLAB*

[Repository](https://github.com/brunojnguerreiro/nova-ieec-m262)

## thesis-template

LaTeX thesis template, based on the one I used for my own PhD thesis. *TeX*
