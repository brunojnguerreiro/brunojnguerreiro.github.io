I am a **Tenured Assistant Professor** at the
[NOVA School of Science and Technology](https://www.fct.unl.pt/) (NOVA FCT),
NOVA University Lisbon, and a researcher with **CTS/UNINOVA/LASI** and the
**Institute for Systems and Robotics** (ISR Lisboa/LARSYS).

I coordinate the MSc in Aerospace Engineering at NOVA FCT, the CTS B2
Research Group and the Cyber-physical and Aerospace Control (CybAer) Lab.

I received my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Instituto
Superior Técnico (IST), Lisbon, in 2013.

## Research interests

My work addresses the theoretical, practical and technological challenges of
aerospace robotics for civilian applications, in particular:

- trajectory planning and tracking for multiple drones
- model predictive control (MPC), linear and nonlinear control
- sensor-based SLAM and navigation
- automatic LiDAR calibration
- modelling and instrumentation of aerial and marine vehicles

## Selected projects

- **PEGASUS** (EU Horizon Europe, 2026–2029): Piloting European Green Air
  Mobility and Services for Urban Societal Readiness.
  [Project site](https://pegasusproject.eu/)
- **CAPTURE** (FCT, 2021–2025, PI): shuttle drone for launch and capture.
  [Project site](http://capture.isr.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/)
- **REPLACE** (FCT, 2018–2022, PI): fast delivery in urban environments using
  drone relays. [Project site](http://replace.isr.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/)
- **MULTIDRONE** (EU H2020, 2017–2019): multiple drone platform for media
  production. [Project site](https://multidrone.eu)
- **SAVEMOB** (FDCT Macau, 2015–2016): sensor-based control of aerial vehicles
  for monitoring industrial facilities.
